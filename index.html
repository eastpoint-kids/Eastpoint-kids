EVERY CHILD WELCOME

We want Eastpoint Kids to be a safe space for YOU 🧩

We want you to feel safe, happy and able to be yourself.

Everyone is different, and that’s brilliant! If things sometimes feel too loud, too busy or a bit overwhelming, we’re here to help.

We have Sensory Support Bags you can borrow. They have things like ear defenders, fidgets and sensory toys inside. Just pick one up from the SEN table and use whatever helps you.

Keep your bag with you while you’re at church, pop everything back inside when you’re finished, and bring it back before you go home.

And if there’s something else that would help you feel safe and comfortable, just tell one of our grown-ups in the yellow T-shirts. We’d love to help.

You know YOU best 💛

Help us get to know you

You can tell us:

  • how you like to communicate
  • things you like or don’t like
  • what helps when feelings get really big
  • what makes you feel safe
  • how you like to join in
  • anything else you’d like us to know

Grown-ups — you can help us with this too! 💛

YOUR QUESTIONS

Things you might want to know

Wondering about something? Have a look below 👇

What should I bring? 🎒

Bring a named water bottle if you have one — and most importantly, bring YOU! 💛 You don’t need anything special. We have sensory things you can use if they help you.

What if I need a little extra help? 💛

Just tell us! We want to get to know YOU — what helps you, what makes things harder, how you like to communicate, and when you might need a break. We’ll do our best to help you feel safe and comfortable. 💛

What if I don't feel like joining in? 💛

That’s completely okay! You can take your time. You don’t have to join in with everything. We’ll help you find a way to be part of Eastpoint Kids that feels comfortable for you. 💛

How will you keep me safe?

Our Eastpoint Kids grown-ups are here to help keep you safe. They are all DBS checked, and there is always a trained First Aider at church. If anything makes you worried, uncomfortable or unsafe, you can always tell one of the adults in a Yellow Tshirt - they're our special helpers on sundays. 💛

Report a safeguarding concern →

Who can I talk to if I need help?

You can talk to any of our Eastpoint Kids grown-ups in a yellow T-shirt. 💛 You can also talk to Michelle or Joy. If you’re worried about something, need some help, or just have a question, you can always tell us.
