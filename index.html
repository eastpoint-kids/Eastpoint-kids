EVERY CHILD WELCOME

A safe space for SEN families 🧩

We want children to be able to join in as themselves. If noise, transitions, communication, crowds or sensory input can be difficult, please tell a member of the team what helps your child.

We can use visual prompts, sensory resources, ear defenders, fidgets and quieter space where available. We’ll work with parents and carers so participation feels safe, predictable and welcoming.

Before Sunday

You’re welcome to tell us about:

  • communication needs
  • sensory triggers
  • what helps when overwhelmed
  • allergies or medical information
  • how your child prefers to join in

FAMILY INFO

Frequently asked questions

What should my child bring?

A named water bottle is useful. If your child uses ear defenders, a comfort item or another familiar sensory aid, please feel free to bring it and let the team know.

What if my child has additional needs?

Please talk to us. We want to understand your child rather than make assumptions. Tell us what helps, what makes things harder, and how they communicate when they need a break.

What if my child doesn’t want to join in?

That’s okay. Children can take time to settle. Our aim is invitation rather than pressure, and we’ll work with you to help them feel comfortable and included.

How do I join the Kids Team?

Speak to one of the Eastpoint leaders and we can explain the safeguarding, DBS, training and team process.
