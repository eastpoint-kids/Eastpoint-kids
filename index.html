EVERY CHILD WELCOME

A safe space for SEN families 🧩

We want everyone to feel welcome, comfortable and able to join in just as they are. We know everyone is different, so please do let us know if there’s anything that would help you or your child feel more at ease.

We have SEN Bags available for both children and adults, with ear defenders, fidgets and sensory resources inside. You’ll find them on the SEN table — please just help yourself to a bag if it would be useful.

All we ask is that you keep the bag with you during the morning, pop everything back inside when you’re finished, and return it to the SEN table before you head home.

If there’s anything else we can do to help make church feel a safe and welcoming space for you or your family, please just ask — we’d love to help.

FAMILY INFO

Frequently asked questions

A few things families often ask us… 💬

What should my child bring?

A named water bottle is useful. Other than that, just bring yourselves! We have SEN bags with ear defenders, fidgets and sensory resources available if they would help.

What if my child has additional needs?

Please talk to us. We want to understand your child rather than make assumptions. Tell us what helps, what makes things harder, and how they communicate when they need a break.

What if my child doesn’t want to join in?

That’s okay. Children can take time to settle. Our aim is invitation rather than pressure, and we’ll work with you to help them feel comfortable and included.

How do you keep children safe?

All of our Eastpoint Kids leaders are DBS checked and follow Eastpoint Vineyard Church’s safeguarding policies and procedures. We also make sure there is a trained First Aider at every service and event we run.

Report a safeguarding concern →

How do I serve on EPVC Kids Team?

Speak to one of the Kids Team, or chat to Michelle or Joy, who jointly coordinate Eastpoint Kids. They’d love to help you find out more about joining the team.
